Last updated: January 12, 2026

Overview

Your privacy matters. Apps developed by Christian Hatch are designed to run entirely on your device and do not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any user data.

Data Collection

None of our apps collect:

  • Personal information
  • Usage or analytics data
  • Device identifiers
  • Diagnostics or crash reports
  • Location data

TrueDepth API & Face Data

Some apps may use Apple's TrueDepth API or other on-device sensors solely for real-time, interactive features (such as facial expression or eye movement detection).

  • No face data or biometric information is collected
  • No face data is stored, logged, retained, or transmitted
  • No face data is shared with third parties
  • All processing occurs locally on the device and is immediately discarded

Data Sharing

We do not share data with third parties. Our apps do not include analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Data Storage & Retention

Because no data is collected, no data is stored or retained, either locally or remotely.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect data from children or users of any age.

Changes to This Policy

If any app's data practices change in the future, this privacy policy will be updated before those changes take effect.
